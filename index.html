Research

Research

I develop interpretable, semi-empirical state-space models to reconstruct monthly terrestrial water-storage anomalies globally. My work combines satellite gravimetry, climate forcings, and physically meaningful latent water stores.

I care not only about predictive skill, but also about whether learned dynamics are scientifically credible: continuous state histories, retention times, reconstruction stability, and transparent causal relationships.
